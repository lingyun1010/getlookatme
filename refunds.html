Refunds & cancellation

Effective 22 September 2026

Real payments are not live in the current Beta. GetLookAtMe does not currently charge your payment method, so there is no paid subscription to refund or cancel.

Current Beta plans

The interface may show Free, Pro, or Founding plan states so Beta limits and upgrade flows can be evaluated. Pro activation uses development-only mock billing and is not a real purchase. A plan label or mock subscription does not create a payment obligation.

Stopping or changing access

You may stop using the Beta at any time. You can unpublish your profile through the product. To request account and data deletion, follow the guidance in the Privacy Policy. The Beta operator may also change or withdraw manually granted access.

Future paid subscriptions

Before real payments begin, this page will be updated with the price shown at checkout, billing interval, how cancellation takes effect, any refund eligibility, and how to contact support. Those terms will apply only after they are presented as part of a real checkout flow; they will not be applied retroactively to this no-charge Beta.

Questions

If a payment-related screen appears to request real card details during the current Beta, do not continue. Report it through the dashboard feedback entry point or the contact route supplied with your Beta invitation.
